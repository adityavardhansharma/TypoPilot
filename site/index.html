How it works

Reads the sentence, not the word.

Conventional checkers often solve one word at a time. TypoPilot reads the damage as a sentence, then makes the smallest correction it can justify.

  1. 01

    Read the whole line

    The full sentence goes in, typos and all. No word is judged alone.

  2. 02

    Infer the intent

    A fine-tuned 1B model uses the surrounding words to decide what you meant.

  3. 03

    Edit minimally

    Only damaged words change. Clean text — and your voice — stay put.
