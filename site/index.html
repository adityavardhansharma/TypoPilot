    02 Method

    It reads the sentence, not the word.

    Conventional checkers judge one word at a time. TypoPilot reads the damage as a whole line, then makes the smallest correction it can justify.

    1. 01

      Read the whole line

      The full sentence goes in, damage and all. Five spans are wrong — and one of them, a lone “c”, is meaningless on its own.

    2. 02

      Let context decide

      Next to “to”, “arrangements” and “party”, that “c” can only be see. The surrounding words resolve what no dictionary could.

    3. 03

      Make the smallest edit

      Five repairs. Every clean word — and the writer’s voice — stays exactly where it was.
