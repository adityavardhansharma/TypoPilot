# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is people frustrated by conventional autocorrect who want a faster, more capable future TypoPilot product. They should understand the promise without knowing machine learning. Researchers and technically curious visitors are a secondary audience served by a dedicated, fully detailed Research page.

## Product Purpose

TypoPilot is currently a model-research project proving that heavily mistyped English can be corrected locally, quickly, and with minimal rewriting. The website should make that future product feel tangible without pretending it is already downloadable, while giving the underlying experiments and results a credible public home.

## Positioning

TypoPilot is a compact local correction model that can recover heavily mistyped sentences with context on an ordinary CPU.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: the hero demonstrates correction directly; the principal explicit route is **Read the research**, followed by deeper benchmark and model-result exploration.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: **Type fast. TypoPilot fixes the rest.**
- Belief ladder: TypoPilot understands damage ordinary autocorrect misses; it corrects with sentence context; it runs locally at useful speed; its claims are backed by inspectable evaluation; it is progressing toward a real product.
- Proof on hand: the 900-example annotated evaluation, the 650-example spelling subset, CPU latency measurements, model comparison reports, training histories, and the 700-example public NSEC companion-corpus evaluation. There are no testimonials or external customer logos yet.

## Brand Personality

Confident, kinetic, and precise. The public-facing voice is optimistic and direct. It leads with strong, defensible achievements; methodological nuance, weaknesses, and negative results remain fully accessible on the Research page.

## Anti-references

The site must not reuse the dark hacker-terminal aesthetic of the existing dashboards and reports. It must not become a generic SaaS card grid, an Apple imitation, an over-rounded glass interface, or a direct copy of Cloudflare. Cloudflare is a reference for commitment, pacing, proof, and completeness—not a template.

## Design Principles

- Demonstrate before explaining: the product promise should visibly happen in the hero.
- Make the pilot metaphor functional: navigation, movement, correction passes, and progress should form one coherent interaction language.
- Lead with confidence, substantiate with evidence: memorable claims must link to their benchmark scope.
- Treat research as a designed experience: experiments, comparisons, limitations, and model history deserve strong information design rather than document dumps.
- Keep the future product tangible without falsely presenting it as available today.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All essential comparisons must remain legible without colour alone. Motion must have a reduced-motion alternative, the correction animation must never block comprehension, keyboard navigation must be complete, and text contrast must meet accessible thresholds.
